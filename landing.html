Frequently Asked Questions

Does Task E require a backend server?

No! Task E runs 100% client-side in the browser using Vanilla JavaScript ES modules. Your tasks are stored safely in your browser's local storage.

Can I use Task E if my browser doesn't support Web Speech API?

Yes! Task E includes a dedicated manual text input drawer that runs through the exact same natural language parser.

How does Google Authentication work?

Task E uses Google Identity Services (GIS). You can sign in using your Google account to personalize your profile and task management.

Can I install Task E on my iPhone or Android?

Yes, Task E is a certified PWA. In Safari on iOS tap "Share" > "Add to Home Screen". On Chrome/Android, tap "Install App" from the browser menu.
